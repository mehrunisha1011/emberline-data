EMBERLINE METHODOLOGY

1. DATA
NASA FIRMS archive, standard-processing active fire detections: MODIS Collection 6.1 (Terra from 2000-11, Aqua from 2002-07), VIIRS 375 m from S-NPP (2012-01) and NOAA-20 (2018-04). The newest few months come from near-real-time processing, not final. Regions finished so far: California, Amazon, Australia, India, Malaysia, Canada boreal.

2. ONE STANDARD FORMAT
Every detection becomes: region, satellite family and platform, latitude, longitude, date, time, fire radiative power (FRP, megawatts), confidence tier, day/night, pixel size, fire type. Only vegetation-fire detections (type 0) are kept, and low-confidence detections are dropped. MODIS confidence (0-100) is converted to three tiers: low (below 30), nominal (30-79), high (80+). VIIRS already reports low/nominal/high. Detections are clipped to each region's bounding box.

3. WHY THERE IS A FAKE JUMP
VIIRS pixels are 375 m, MODIS pixels are 1 km, so VIIRS detects smaller fires. When VIIRS starts in 2012, detection counts jump even if nothing changed on the ground. Smaller steps happen when Aqua (2002) and NOAA-20 (2018) start, because more satellite passes mean more chances to see a fire.

4. HOW MUCH DOES MODIS MISS?
Aqua (MODIS) and S-NPP (VIIRS) cross the same place at nearly the same time of day. For each daytime S-NPP detection (near-nadir pixels only, so Aqua could physically see it) we look for an Aqua detection on the same day, within 60 minutes, whose pixel footprint contains it. The share that Aqua also saw, by fire-power bin, is the detection curve. We match only 2013-2022, because Aqua's overpass time drifts later after 2022.
Even for the largest fires Aqua does not catch everything (clouds, smoke, viewing geometry), so the threshold is relative: the smallest FRP above which MODIS catches at least 90% of what it catches for big fires (the plateau).
Per region (threshold, plateau): California 50 MW (plateau 0.78); Amazon 50 MW (plateau 0.54); Australia 30 MW (plateau 0.68); India 50 MW (plateau 0.69); Malaysia 100 MW (plateau 0.64); Canada boreal 30 MW (plateau 0.66).

5. THE FIX
The fixed series keeps only detections at or above the region's threshold, for MODIS and VIIRS alike, so every year measures the same size of fire. Each ~1 km cell is counted once per day however many satellites saw it. 2000-2002 (Terra only) is scaled by the Terra-plus-Aqua to Terra-only ratio measured in 2003-2005.

6. FIRE EVENTS
Detections are collapsed to ~1 km cell-days (same fair set as the fixed series), then linked into one event when within 3 km and 2 days of each other. Each event has start, peak (day of highest total FRP), end, total FRP and the satellites that saw it. Events below 50 MW total FRP or 3 cell-days are dropped, and only the 300 biggest events per year are exported per region to keep the files small.

7. RESULT: THE 2012 STEP
We compare the 2012 step to a control: the same step measured on MODIS alone, which has no instrument change in 2012. Excess = (1 + step) / (1 + control) - 1.
- California: raw +303% (MODIS-only control +72%, excess +134%); fixed +149% (control +108%, excess +20%)
- Amazon: raw +172% (MODIS-only control -12%, excess +209%); fixed +8% (control -19%, excess +32%)
- Australia: raw +208% (MODIS-only control +13%, excess +171%); fixed +45% (control +15%, excess +27%)
- India: raw +410% (MODIS-only control +1%, excess +407%); fixed +27% (control -7%, excess +37%)
- Malaysia: raw +295% (MODIS-only control -2%, excess +303%); fixed +4% (control -17%, excess +26%)
- Canada boreal: raw +416% (MODIS-only control +51%, excess +242%); fixed +141% (control +50%, excess +60%)
Burned-area validation (MCD64A1) has not been done yet.

8. LIMITATIONS
- FRP is fire intensity, not area burned.
- Clouds and heavy smoke hide fires from both satellites (Amazon plateau is lowest).
- Aqua's overpass time drifts after 2022, so matching uses 2013-2022 only.
- The threshold is estimated from daytime data and applied to day and night.
- The size threshold removes small fires from the fixed series for both sensors.
- The newest months mix near-real-time and standard processing.
- Only the regions listed above are processed; the rest are pending.
